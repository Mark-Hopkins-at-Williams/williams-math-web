The 86th MAA Putnam Competition will be held on **Saturday, December 6th**.

The Putnam Competition is a very prestigious, and arguably the most difficult, math competition for undergraduate students, where the median score is usually 0 or 1 (out of 120). This means if you successfully attempt one question (not even solve, but attempt), you will outscore more than half of the students in the country. There is nothing to lose and so many ego points to gain, so why would you not join?

Sign up by December 5th to participate! If you would like to know more about the competition, please find Professor Ian Seong (is11@williams.edu, Wachenheim 333).
