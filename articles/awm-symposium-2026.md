The Williams College Student Chapter of the Association for Women in Mathematics (AWM) is excited to be hosting a one-day regional symposium to bring together undergraduate and graduate students and faculty from across New England.

The symposium will take place at Williams College on **Saturday, March 7th, 2026**.

Our plenary speakers are Amanda Folsom (Amherst College) and Becca Thomases (Smith College). We will also have parallel sessions for students to give short 20-minute talks on research or topics of interest. More information is available on the [symposium schedule](https://sites.google.com/williams.edu/awm/symposium-2026/schedule).
