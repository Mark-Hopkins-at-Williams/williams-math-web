In some cases, and with prior permission of the Mathematics and Statistics Department, appropriate courses from other institutions or a course from another Williams department may be substituted for electives. Programs like the "Budapest Semester in Mathematics" are recommended for majors who wish to focus on mathematics away, but the department normally accommodates students who select other study away programs. The department offers its core courses in both the fall and the spring to make it easier to spend a semester away.

Students **must** contact the department before assuming study away credit will be granted toward the major. A few things to know:

- **Pre-approval:** the department can pre-approve courses in many cases, but be sure to contact us.
- **What we need:** course title and description, and a complete syllabus including readings and assignments.
- **Limits:** there is no limit on the number of courses, but they must be approved as MATH/STAT courses.
- **What can't be done away:** the colloquium requirement and the senior seminar requirement.
- **Plan ahead:** keep in mind the highly cumulative structure of the major.
