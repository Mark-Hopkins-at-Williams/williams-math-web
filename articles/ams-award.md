The American Mathematical Society has recognized the Williams College Department of Mathematics and Statistics as the 2014 Exemplary Program, as [announced](https://www.ams.org/notices/201405/rnoti-p517.pdf) and [described](https://www.ams.org/notices/201405/rnoti-p518.pdf) in the May 2014 *Notices of the AMS*. See also the [College press release](https://today.williams.edu/announcements/4_10_2014_amsaward/).
