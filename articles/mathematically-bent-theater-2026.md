**Mathematically Bent Theater**, on Zoom, Monday, April 20, 6:30-7:30pm.

Four short humorous mathematically bent pieces, written and performed by Colin Adams (Williams faculty) and starring Josh Cohen (Williams student), Jennifer Beineke (Western New England University faculty), Thomas Kindred (Smith College faculty and Williams grad), and Jenna Zomback (Amherst faculty and past faculty at Williams).

Sponsored by the Museum of Mathematics in NYC, with host Arthur Benjamin. Free registration was available through the Museum of Mathematics.
