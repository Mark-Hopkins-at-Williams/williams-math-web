The Putnam Competition is back! To prepare for the competition that is in December, or to simply have fun solving challenging problems, check out the Problem Solving Nights:

- **Time:** 7-8pm every other Tuesday
- **Location:** Wachenheim 234

The first session is on September 22, and the other dates are October 6 and 20, November 3 and 17, and December 1.

The sessions will consist of a variety of problems, from very manageable to IMO/Putnam-level hard. Even if you are not planning to participate in the Putnam, feel free to come along, solve problems, make friends, and most importantly, there will be snacks! If you have any questions about these sessions, please contact Professor Ian Seong (is11@williams.edu).
