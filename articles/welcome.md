Welcome to the Williams College Department of Mathematics. Mathematics majors learn with faculty how to think, how to understand, and how to solve problems. Every major gives a full colloquium talk and participates in a senior seminar. Many majors take tutorials and do original research. We all have a lot of fun together.

Majors typically go on in mathematics, statistics, economics, other sciences, engineering, law, medicine, business, finance, consulting, teaching, and other careers.

The American Mathematical Society has recognized the Williams College Department of Mathematics and Statistics as the [2014 Exemplary Program](https://www.ams.org/notices/201405/rnoti-p518.pdf).
